The colour salon / an observation guide

Which Cavalier coat do you see?

Look at the coat in neutral daylight. Follow three observations to compare it with the four colour descriptions. This identifies a visible pattern, not breed identity, pedigree or genetics.

Observation 1 of 3

Start with the white areas

Your observation card

This is a descriptive match, not an official assessment. Small white marks and unclear observations need a closer comparison rather than a forced colour label.

The four descriptions

Blenheim

Chestnut patches on a white ground.

Ruby

A rich red coat throughout.

Tricolour

Black and white with tan markings.

Black and Tan

Black with tan markings, without a white ground.

Source: Royal Kennel Club — Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Colour section. Descriptions here are simplified for observation, rather than show judging.
